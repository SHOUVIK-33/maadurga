Navratri ke nau dinon ki sampoorn puja — Ghatasthapana (Kalash sthapana) ki vidhi aur muhurat, din-wise shubh rang, samagri checklist, aur Kanya Pujan tatha Hawan ki vidhi.

॥ नौ रंग ॥

Color of the Day (Navratri 2026)

Har din ka ek shubh rang — kapdon aur puja mein istemal karein.

॥ घटस्थापना ॥

Ghatasthapana (Kalash Sthapana) Vidhi

Navratri ke pehle din kalash sthapit karne ki step-by-step vidhi.

  1. Snan aur Sankalp: Subah snan karke saf vastra pehnein. Puja-sthal ko ganga-jal se shuddh karein aur Maa ka dhyan karke sankalp lein.
  2. Chowki aur Aasan: Ek chowki par laal ya safed vastra bichhayein. Us par Maa ki murti ya chitra sthapit karein.
  3. Kalash Sthapana: Mitti ke kalash mein saaf mitti daalein aur jau ke beej boyein. Kalash ke muh par aam ke patte rakhein aur us par nariyal sthapit karein.
  4. Kalava aur Chunri: Kalash par laal kalava bandhein aur Maa ko laal chunri arpit karein.
  5. Diya aur Dhoop: Ghee ka diya jalayein, dhoop-agarbatti karein aur ghanti-shankh bajaayein.
  6. Pushp aur Bhog: Maa ko phool, akshat aur din-anusaar bhog arpit karein. Navadurga mantra ka jaap karein.
  7. Aarti: Aarti utarkar prasad grahan karein. Aise hi nau din tak (aur akhand-jyoti ho to nirantar) puja karein.
Ghatasthapana Muhurat (2026):
Shardiya Navratri — 11 October 2026, Ghatasthapana Muhurat subah 6:19 AM – 10:12 AM; Abhijit Muhurat 11:44 AM – 12:31 PM.
Niyam: Kalash sthapana ke samay Sthir aur Shubh muhurat dekhein. Ghatasthapana ke baad jau (barley) ugana shubh maana jaata hai — ise 'Khetri' ya 'Jaware' kehte hain.
Akhand Jyoti: Navratri mein akhand jyoti (nirantar diya) jalana mangal-karak hai. Iska dhyan rakhein ki jyoti bujhe na aur safai ka khyal rahe.
॥ पूजा सामग्री ॥

Samagri Checklist

Hawan, Puja aur Kanya Pujan ke liye zaroori saari samagri.

॥ कन्या पूजन ॥

Kanya Pujan & Hawan Guide

Vidhi-vidhan se Kanya Pujan aur Hawan mein ahuti dene ke niyam.

Kanya Pujan Vidhi

  1. Ashtami ya Navami ko 2 se 10 varsh ki 9 kanyaon (aur 1 chhote balak) ko snan karाकर saaf vastra pehnaayein.
  2. Unke paanv dhovakar, charan-sparsh karke unhe Maa ka swaroop maan kar aasan par bithayein.
  3. Roli-kalava, chunri, phool aur itra arpit karein; unki puja karein.
  4. Bhog mein kala chana, poori, halwa aur phal arpit karein.
  5. Kanyaon ke paanv chhukar aashirwad lein aur dakshina/upahaar dekar vida karein.

Hawan Vidhi

  1. Hawan kund mein samidha (aam ki lakdi) aur hawan samagri sthapit karein.
  2. Ghee aur samagri se hawan prarambh karein — pehli ahuti Maa Durga ke naam.
  3. Navarna mantra ya Durga Saptashati ke shlok padhte hue ahuti dein.
  4. Chandi Homa mein 700 ahuti (Saptashati ke 700 shlokon ke anusaar) di jaati hain.
  5. Ant mein purnahuti aur aarti karke prasad grahan karein.

Puja-vidhi sthaan, parampara aur sampraday ke hisaab se thodi badal sakti hai. Vishesh anushthan ke liye apne kula-guru ya anubhavi pandit ji se margdarshan lein.

Navratri Bhakti

Puja vidhi, samagri aur Kanya Pujan ki sampoorn jaankari.

॥ जय माता दी ॥
