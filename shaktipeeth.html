51 Shaktipeeth & Mandir Directory५१ शक्तिपीठ और प्रमुख मंदिर

Pauranik manyata ke anusaar, Daksh-yagya ke baad Sati ne deh tyaag diya. Bhagwan Vishnu ne Sudarshan Chakra se unke sharir ko vibhajit kiya, aur jahan-jahan unka ang ya aabhushan gira, wahan Shaktipeeth sthapit hue. Har peeth mein Devi ki ek swaroop aur unke saath Bhairav (Shiv ka swaroop) virajit hain.

॥ भू-दर्शन ॥

Poore 51 Shaktipeeth

Naam se search karein ya desh ke hisaab se filter karein.

Sroton mein peethon ke naam, ang/aabhushan aur Bhairav ke naam mein thoda matbhed milta hai — yahan sarvadhik prachalit manyata di gayi hai.
