Navratri & Seasonal Significanceनवरात्रि और उसका महत्व

Navratri ka arth hai 'nau raat'. Saal mein chaar Navratri aati hain — do pramukh (Sharad aur Chaitra) aur do Gupt (Magha aur Ashadha). Har Navratri ka apna pauranik aadhar, rit-vaigyanik mahatva aur aadhyatmik uddeshya hai.

॥ तिथि पंचांग ॥

Navratri Dates (2026 & 2027)

Neeche di gayi tithiyan poore Bharat ke liye samanya hain. Ghatasthapana aur Ashtami/Navami ka sahi samay apne sthaan ke panchang se zaroor milaayein.

Dhyan dein: 2026 mein Shardiya Navratri 11 October se 20 October tak hai (Dussehra 20 October). Tithi sunrise ke hisaab se badalti hai, isliye kuch shehron mein Ashtami-Navami ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.

॥ चार नवरात्रि ॥

Saal ki 4 Navratri ka Bhed

Har Navratri ka naam, samay aur uddeshya alag hai.

॥ पौराणिक कथा ॥

Mahishasura Vadh ki Katha

Navratri ke peeche ki purani katha — Markandeya Puran ke anusaar.

Mahishasura ka Vardaan

Mahishasura (mahish = bhains) naam ke asur ne kathin tapasya se Brahma ji ko prasann kiya aur aisa vardaan maanga ki use na koi devta, na manushya, na asur maar sake — kewal ek stri hi usse vadh kar sake. Yeh sunkar veh atyant ahankar mein aa gaya aur teenon lokon par atyachar karne laga.

Maa Durga ka Prakat Hona

Devtaon ke tej (Brahma, Vishnu, Mahesh aur anya devtaon ke tejas) se ek divya stri-roop prakat hua — Maa Durga. Unhe Shiv ne trishul, Vishnu ne chakra, Indra ne vajra, aur anya devtaon ne apne-apne shastra diye. Maa ne simha (sher) par sawar hokar asuron se yuddh kiya.

Nau Raat ka Yuddh

Nav raat tak chala bhayanak yuddh. Aathvi raat (Ashtami) ko Maa ne Mahishasura ko parajit kiya aur navami ko ushka vadh kiya. Isi vijay ki smriti mein Navratri aur Dussehra manaya jaata hai — burai par acchai ki jeet.

Raktabija aur Shumbh-Nishumbh

Devi Mahatmya ke aage ke adhyaayon mein Maa ne Raktabija (jiski har boond se naya asur ban jaata tha), Chanda-Munda aur Shumbh-Nishumbh ka bhi vadh kiya. Inhi vijayon ke saath Navratri ka paath juda hai.

॥ ऋतु विज्ञान ॥

Seasonal Science — Vrat ke Swasthya Labh

Ritu-parivartan ke samay upvas rakhna keval aadhyatmik nahi, vaigyanik drishti se bhi labhdayak hai.

Zaroori suchna: Upvas ek vyaktigat nirnay hai. Diabetes, blood pressure, pregnancy ya kisi bhi bimari mein doctor ki salaah zaroor lein. Yeh jaankari keval samanya hai, medical advice nahi.

Navratri Bhakti

Navratri aur Maa Durga ke sampoorn viṣay par saral aur shraddhapurn guide.

॥ जय माता दी ॥
